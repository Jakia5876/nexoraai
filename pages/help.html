Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use my existing personal or business phone number?

+
Yes! You can connect your existing WhatsApp Business or standard number in seconds via QR scan. For official Meta Cloud API, you can port any existing mobile or landline number that is able to receive a 6-digit OTP verification call.

Does NEXORAAI support the Bengali language?

+
Absolutely! Our AI models are fine-tuned to comprehend and reply fluently in formal Bangla (বাংলা), conversational everyday Bengali, and Romanized "Banglish" script.

What payment methods do you accept for subscriptions?

+
We accept all major International Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX) via Stripe, as well as local Bangladesh payments through bKash, Nagad, and Rocket auto-debit.

How does NEXORAAI prevent WhatsApp numbers from getting banned?

+
NEXORAAI incorporates enterprise-grade human emulation: randomized jitter delays between 4 to 9 seconds, automatic suppression of recipients who opt-out, and pre-warmed sending ramp algorithms.
