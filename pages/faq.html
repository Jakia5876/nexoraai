Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up NEXORAAI, WhatsApp verification, AI personas, billing, and developer APIs.

Can I connect multiple WhatsApp numbers to one workspace?
Yes! Depending on your subscription plan, NEXORAAI supports multiple active WhatsApp sessions simultaneously. Starter includes 1 session, Professional includes 3 sessions, Business includes 10 sessions, and Enterprise provides custom unlimited numbers. Each number can be assigned distinct AI agents, departments, and routing rules.
Do I need a verified Facebook Business Manager to start?
You can test instantly in developer sandbox mode using standard web sessions or test Meta Cloud API credentials. For official enterprise production broadcasts and blue checkmark verification, a verified Meta Business Manager account with Cloud API access is recommended. Our team guides you through the entire verification process.
How does the AI ensure it doesn't give incorrect prices or terms?
NEXORAAI utilizes strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The AI agent is grounded solely in the PDFs, URLs, catalogs, and FAQ documents you upload to your private knowledge base. If a customer asks a question outside your approved documentation, the AI automatically executes your configured fallback response or seamlessly hands off the chat to a human agent.
Can human agents take over a live AI conversation?
Yes, at any moment. The Unified Inbox provides real-time chat monitoring. An agent can click "Pause AI" or type a message directly, which temporarily suspends the autonomous AI responder for that conversation. The AI can also automatically trigger a handoff when confidence drops below your defined threshold.
What payment methods are supported in Bangladesh and globally?
We support regional mobile financial services (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and SSLCommerz) in Bangladeshi Taka (৳ BDT) as well as international cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) through Stripe in USD. Automated tax invoices are generated upon successful payment.
Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription directly from your Workspace Billing dashboard. When upgrading, charges are prorated. If you cancel, your subscription remains active until the end of your prepaid billing cycle.
How does the Lead Research feature work?
NEXORAAI integrates with authorized business registries, public API directories, and your imported CSV datasets. You can query target categories and cities (e.g., "restaurants in Dhaka", "dentists in Chittagong") to enrich verified phone numbers, websites, and business emails directly into your CRM pipeline without violating anti-bot terms.
How is tenant data isolated and protected?
Every database table enforces Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) linked strictly to your organization_id. API secrets are never stored in plaintext — only cryptographically salted hashes are retained. Communication across sessions, sockets, and webhooks is protected with TLS 1.3 encryption.
Can I connect NEXORAAI to my own n8n or Zapier instance?
Yes! NEXORAAI features first-class n8n and REST API integrations. You can dispatch outbound messages, subscribe to 12+ real-time webhook events (messages, lead status, payments), and import pre-built JSON workflow templates into self-hosted n8n in seconds.
