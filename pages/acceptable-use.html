Compliance & Standards

Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Applies to all NEXORAAI accounts and integrations

Policy Objective

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules and restrictions governing the use of NEXORAAI's WhatsApp API, AI Automation agents, Lead Intelligence tools, and webhook infrastructure. By accessing NEXORAAI, you agree to strictly follow these standards.

1. WhatsApp Official Business Compliance

NEXORAAI is committed to preserving high message delivery quality and recipient trust. All customers must comply with:

  • Explicit Consent (Opt-in): You may only initiate WhatsApp conversations with contacts who have affirmatively consented to receive messages from your organization.
  • Clear Opt-out Mechanism: Every campaign or broadcast message must honor unsubscribe requests (e.g. keywords such as STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, or Opt-Out buttons).
  • Approved Message Templates: Promotional messages initiated by the business outside the 24-hour service window must utilize Meta-approved WhatsApp Message Templates.

2. Prohibited Content and Activities

You may not use NEXORAAI to transmit, process, or automate any of the following:

  • Unsolicited bulk spam or unsolicited cold marketing messages to individuals without prior consent.
  • Phishing, credential harvesting, social engineering, or fraudulent financial schemes.
  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, defamatory content, or non-consensual personal data distribution.
  • Illegal products, narcotics, unlicensed pharmaceuticals, counterfeit merchandise, or unauthorized gambling.
  • Malware, viruses, trojans, or automated scripts designed to disrupt external infrastructure.

3. Lead Intelligence & Data Research Compliance

NEXORAAI provides lead enrichment and research tooling designed for verified business-to-business (B2B) communications. Under this policy:

  • You agree NOT to use automated scraping that bypasses CAPTCHA, anti-bot mechanisms, authentication walls, or technical rate restrictions.
  • Lead research must rely exclusively on authorized business-data providers, legitimate public registries, or customer-provided opt-in CSV datasets.
  • Enriched lead information must be handled in compliance with applicable data privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and regional personal data protection acts.

4. AI Agent & Automation Conduct

When deploying autonomous AI chat agents and automated responders:

  • AI agents must accurately identify themselves as automated systems when asked by human users.
  • Automated workflows must provide a seamless handoff to human support upon customer request or escalation.
  • You are responsible for the truthfulness and accuracy of all responses generated from your uploaded knowledge bases.

5. Rate Limits & Infrastructure Protection

Users must adhere to system concurrency limits, API rate quotas, and broadcast interval safeguards. Any intentional attempt to bypass rate-limiting, exploit vulnerabilities, or degrade platform availability will result in immediate suspension.

6. Enforcement and Violations

We review abuse reports and automated spam health metrics in real time. NEXORAAI reserves the right to suspend or permanently terminate any workspace found violating this policy without refund.
