Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

NEXORAAI collects necessary business information to provide our WhatsApp API, automated chat processing, and CRM services. This includes company name, account credentials, telephone numbers, and message logs necessary for transit and delivery verification.

2. Meta Platform Compliance

NEXORAAI complies with the Meta WhatsApp Business Terms of Service and Cloud API policies. We do not sell, rent, or distribute recipient contact databases to any third parties.

3. AI Data Privacy

Customer proprietary documents uploaded to the Knowledge Base are vectorized strictly for your dedicated workspace tenant. Your private data is never used to train public foundational LLM models.

4. Data Security

All in-transit data is encrypted with TLS 1.3. API keys and webhook secrets are stored with irreversible bcrypt/Argon2 hashing.
